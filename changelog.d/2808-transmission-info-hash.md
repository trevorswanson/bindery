### Fixed
- **Transmission downloads survive a daemon restart** (#2808): Bindery stored the numeric id Transmission hands out when a torrent is added, and Transmission renumbers every torrent when it restarts. After a restart a download could sit at downloading for good, or have its status, import and queue removal land on whichever torrent inherited the number, including deleting that torrent's data. Transmission downloads are now tracked by info hash, which never changes. Downloads grabbed before this release are matched back to their torrent on the next poll by when they were added, with the release name breaking ties, and are only rewritten when the match is unambiguous. One that cannot be matched is never removed by its old id: remove it in Transmission yourself. Thanks dClauzel for the report and ccarpinteri for the fix.
